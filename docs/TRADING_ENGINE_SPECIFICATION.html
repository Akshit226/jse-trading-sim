1 The 4-Stage Trade Lifecycle

When a student enters a quantity and clicks BUY or SELL on their trading pad, the engine processes the order through four instantaneous phases:

01 Validation

Verifies available cash margin or share balance to eliminate uncollateralized risk.

02 Execution

Atomic swap: liquid cash deducted/credited and share inventory adjusted.

03 Price Impact

Calculates buying/selling pressure based on capital volume vs stock depth.

04 Mark-to-Market

Recalculates room-wide portfolio Net Worth, Return %, and Projector ranks.

2 Mathematical Calculations & Formulas

A. Order Validation & Capital Requirement

The total capital required to execute a trade is computed as:

Total Cost = Quantity × Current Market Price (CMP)

The Gatekeeper Constraints:

  • For BUY Orders: Liquid Cash ≥ Total Cost. If cash is insufficient, rejected with error: "Insufficient cash. Need ₹X, but have ₹Y".
  • For SELL Orders: Shares Owned ≥ Quantity. Prevents illegal short selling. Rejected with error: "Insufficient shares. Attempted to sell X, but hold Y".

B. Dynamic Market Impact & Price Shift

In real financial markets, trading volume moves the price based on available liquidity. The simulator implements a linear capital depth model:

Price Shift % = Order Value in ₹ / Stock Liquidity Depth (D)
BUY Order Dynamic

Generates upward buying pressure, shifting the price UP:

New Price = CMP × (1 + Price Shift %)
SELL Order Dynamic

Injects supply into the market, pulling the price DOWN:

New Price = CMP × (1 - Price Shift %)
Protective Circuit Breakers:
  • Single-Trade 8% Shift Cap: No individual order can move a stock by more than ±8.0% in a single click, preventing market manipulation.
  • Absolute ₹10.00 Price Floor: A stock can never crash below ₹10.00.

C. Portfolio Net Worth & Leaderboard Ranks

Every student's wealth is evaluated Mark-to-Market every second:

Equity Value = Σ (Shares Held × Current Stock Price)
Total Net Worth = Liquid Cash + Equity Value
Return % = [ (Total Net Worth - ₹10,00,000) / ₹10,00,000 ] × 100%

• Stock: COFORGE (Depth = ₹35,00,000). Current Price: ₹7,600.00.

Calculation Step Formula Applied Resulting Figure
1. Order Cost 10 shares × ₹7,600.00 ₹76,000.00
2. Cash Deduction ₹6,00,000 - ₹76,000 ₹5,24,000.00
3. Shares Added 0 + 10 shares 10 shares

5 Real Market (NSE) vs. Classroom Simulator
